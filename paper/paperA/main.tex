\title{DriftBench-CF: Counterfactual Fork Labels for Goal Drift in Long-Horizon Agents}
\author{Muzra Fatima}

\begin{abstract}
Agent-monitoring benchmarks record what happened, not what would have happened under intervention. We
release DriftBench-CF, a goal-drift benchmark whose labels come from seed-paired forks. For every
labelled prefix it gives rollout-grounded violation risk and, for four goal re-anchoring strengths, the
rescue and disruption probabilities. Version 1 covers 4 environments, 11 synthetic agents and 5 drift
types: 220 runs and 1,179 labelled prefixes. Fork noise is calibrated (predicted SE 0.145 against an
observed replicate SD of 0.137), and effect heterogeneity is significant for every intervention strength.
\end{abstract}
